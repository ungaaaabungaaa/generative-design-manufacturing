A / THE SEARCH

Seeded, bounded, repeatable.

Mutation and crossover change section heights. Repair enforces the height grid and adjacent steps. Elitism retains the feasible baseline. Every candidate is scored with the beam evaluator.

B / THE COMPARISON

A stated denominator.

The baseline is the smallest feasible uniform height on the same grid, under the same load, span, material inputs, and limits. Volume reduction is divided by that baseline's volume.

C / THE PHYSICS

An explicit approximation.

Piecewise Euler–Bernoulli integration estimates tip deflection and nominal bending stress. The evaluator is checked against constant-section closed forms and independent numerical integration.

D / THE BOUNDARY

A study, before a part.

The model omits step stress concentrations, shear, fatigue, buckling, fixtures, and process tolerances. These results are not FEA, global optimality, structural certification, or a tested physical build.
