# Sources and attribution

The mechanics model follows standard beam-bending and unit-load/energy relations. Reference: [MIT, Deflections due to Bending](https://web.mit.edu/1.105/emech10_04.pdf). No textbook prose, source code, or figure was copied. The evaluator, search, geometry exports, and viewer are original implementations for this study. Figures are generated from the real report.

Optional plotting uses Matplotlib and optional independent DXF validation uses ezdxf under their own licenses. Neither is bundled as a runtime dependency. No dataset or trained model is included.

The original compressed plotter tooling and that source remain archived unchanged. They are not used by the new study, and the new license does not relicense archived material.
